## Brand & Style

The brand personality is contemplative, aristocratic, and sensorially evocative. It reflects the meticulous precision of a Parisian bespoke fragrance atelier fused with sun-drenched Mediterranean botanical landscapes. The target audience comprises discerning patrons, connoisseurs of niche perfumery, and collectors seeking rare olfactory compositions and artisanal heritage.

The emotional response sought is quiet reverie, tactile indulgence, and uncompromising luxury. Interactions should feel deliberate, peaceful, and elevated, eschewing fast-paced retail urgency in favor of cultural storytelling and olfactory exploration.

The design movement synthesizes **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with classical French print traditions:
- Generous, curated white-and-ivory negative space that mirrors museum galleries.
- Whispering structural elements marked by hairline rules (1px), subtle champagne accents, and immaculate balance.
- Tactile warmth replacing sterile digital grays with organic limestone, cream, and antique gold tones.

## Colors

The color architecture is built entirely upon natural atelier materials: hand-milled vellum paper, aged brass hardware, cold-pressed essences, and oxidized charcoal ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#B69A68` - Antique Gold):** Reserved for focal gestures, signature CTA buttons, verified botanic stamps, active indicators, and gilded hover states.
- **Secondary (`#D9C5A3` - Champagne Beige):** Applied to ornamental structural separators, subtle background fills, borders on focus states, and warm decorative lines.
- **Tertiary (`#817B72` - Warm Grey):** Governs secondary metadata, olfactory pyramid notes (top, heart, base), copyright markers, and disabled states while preserving warmth.
- **Neutral Dark (`#25231F` - Deep Charcoal):** Serves as the primary typographic ink and icon shade, offering high-contrast readability without the clinical harshness of absolute `#000000`.
- **Surfaces (`#F8F5EF`, `#F1E9DC`, `#FFFFFF`):** The primary canvas rests on warm ivory (`#F8F5EF`), secondary modules and drawers recede into soft cream (`#F1E9DC`), and floating inspection cards lift onto pure white (`#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances dramatic, romantic literary cadence with ultra-crisp modern legibility.

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Narratives):** Carries the legacy of classical French publishing. Utilized for collection titles, fragrance designations, poetic accords, and philosophical brand passages. Display sizes utilize tight leading to emphasize sculptural serifs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Functional UI, & Metadata):** A contemporary sans-serif providing crystal-clear legibility at compact sizes. When used in all-caps labels, category names, or navigation, it strictly enforces expansive tracking (`0.15em` to `0.25em`) to evoke traditional luxury house engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is that of an open, airy atelier portfolio. Negative space is treated as an active luxury material rather than void.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 2rem (32px) gutters and 4rem (64px) minimum outer canvas margins. Centered container caps at a maximum width of 1440px. Editorial storytelling sections alternate between 6-column pairs and offset 8-column centerpieces.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem (24px) gutters and 2.5rem (40px) outer margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem (16px) gutters and 1.5rem (24px) margins. Grids collapse sequentially into single or asymmetric double columns.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component vertical flow respects multiples of 8px. Spacing between distinct editorial chapters is deliberately wide (`space-xl` scaled to double intervals) to encourage deliberate contemplation during scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows and skeuomorphic bevels, choosing instead the tactile delicacy of fine paper and ambient diffusion.

### Hierarchy via Tonal Layering & Ghost Borders
- **Depth Tier 0 (The Ground):** Raw canvas `#F8F5EF`. Completely matte, grounding all editorial narratives.
- **Depth Tier 1 (Architectural Panels):** Modules, product showcase panels, and drawer sections use `#F1E9DC` framed with a subtle 1px border colored with `#D9C5A3` at 40% to 60% opacity.
- **Depth Tier 2 (Floating Receptacles & Modals):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` canvases paired with ultra-diffused ambient luminescence:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(37, 35, 31, 0.06), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(37, 35, 31, 0.02);`
  - The drop-off is wide, soft, and tinted with `#25231F` (Deep Charcoal) rather than a neutral black, producing the warm cast of natural afternoon light through atelier windows.
- **Depth Tier 3 (Tactile Hover & Selection):** Subtle micro-lift (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an antique gold outer ring or hairline accentuation (`1px solid #B69A68`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, restrained shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Corners across buttons, inputs, structural frames, and image containers carry a discreet radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- This microscopic rounding softens technical digital geometry while maintaining the architectural precision of heavy cut crystal, tailored bookbinding, and beveled flacon glass.
- Specialized contextual tags (such as fragrance notes and olfactory tags) utilize a slightly softened contour (`rounded-lg` at 0.5rem / 8px), preserving an artisanal pressed-paper appearance without sliding into informal pill shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#25231F`, text `#F8F5EF`, 1px border `#25231F`. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Text style: `label-uppercase`. Subtle hover transition shifts background to `#B69A68` and border to `#B69A68`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background transparent, text `#25231F`, 1px border `#D9C5A3`. Hover transitions background to `#F1E9DC` and border to `#B69A68`.
- **Text Action:** Clean underlined text in `label-uppercase` with an offset hairline underline in `#B69A68`.

### Chips & Fragrance Tags
- Compact label containers displaying fragrance notes (e.g., *ORANGE BLOSSOM*, *AMBER RESIN*, *BERGAMOT*).
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F1E9DC`, 1px border `#D9C5A3`, text `#817B72` set in `label-uppercase`. Active or selected tags shift to `#25231F` background with `#F8F5EF` text.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Display Card:** Set against `#FFFFFF` or transparent canvas with a 1px border (`#D9C5A3` at 40%). Image aspect ratio 4:5. Features title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/accords in `body-sm` (`#817B72`), and price formatted in `price-tag`. Hover triggers a smooth 1.02x scale zoom on fragrance bottles and surfaces a discreet quick-view trigger.
- **Accord Showcase Card:** Minimalist square cards detailing the pyramid (Head, Heart, Base) with fine golden lines connecting olfactory tiers.

### Form Inputs
- Minimalist text boxes with transparent backgrounds and an ambient bottom hairline (`1px solid #D9C5A3`) or full 1px perimeter box.
- Floating placeholder set in `body-sm` (`#817B72`).
- Focus state illuminates border to `#B69A68` with no harsh outline glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square or circular form elements (16px by 16px) bounded by a 1px `#D9C5A3` perimeter. Checked state features a centered micro-square or dot rendered in `#B69A68`.

### Lists & Accordions
- Bespoke accordions for ingredient provenance, notes, and ritual application. Separated by delicate 1px borders in `#E6DDCE`. Expansion reveals smooth vertical unfolding with typography set in `body-md`.
